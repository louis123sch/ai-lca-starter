[PARAGRAPH 9]
For the background system, data on multifunctional processes were obtained from the Ecoinvent v3.6 cut-off model. Datasets were chosen by regional allocation priority. For domestic H2 production in Germany, the priority was Germany (DE), Europe (RER), Europe without Switzerland (EWS), global (GLO), and rest of the world (RoW). For country-specific results, the datasets of natural gas and electricity supply were adapted to the grid mixes in the respective country.
