The study evaluates six hydrogen production pathways in Canada using a well-to-gate system boundary. The functional unit/reference flow is 1 kg of compressed gaseous hydrogen with purity >=99.9%, default compression pressure 62.2 bar, and LHV 119.9 MJ/kg. The six pathways are: alkaline electrolysis (AE), proton exchange membrane electrolysis (PEM), biomass gasification with carbon capture, steam methane reforming (SMR) without carbon capture, SMR with carbon capture, and autothermal reforming (ATR) with carbon capture.

Supplementary Tables S1-S7 provide LCI datasets. Table S6 (ATR without carbon capture) is supplementary inventory data but is not one of the six pathways assessed in the main study and should not be promoted into the foreground benchmark system.
