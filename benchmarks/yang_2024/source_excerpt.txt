[DOCUMENT: Yang et al. 2024, Science of the Total Environment 920, 171065]

The study compares two hydrogen production routes with a cradle-to-gate system boundary and a functional unit of producing 1 kg of hydrogen. The two assessed LCA product systems are DSSW alkaline-catalysed hydrolysis (DACH) and alkaline water electrolysis (AEL). Review discussion of PEM and SOEC is descriptive and is not part of the assessed foreground LCA.

DACH is assessed during the hydrogen-production process only. It contains two main inventory stages: removal of surface oxides, and hydrolysis/hydrogen production. Reaction-vessel impacts are excluded.

Table 1. Life-cycle inventory of DACH for 1 kg H2.
Oxide removal inputs: DSSW 5.71 kg; hydrofluoric acid 0.14 kg; ethanol 18.02 kg; deionized water 67.25 kg. Oxide removal output: DSSW (oxide removal) 5.54 kg.
Hydrolysis/hydrogen-production inputs: DSSW (oxide removal) 5.54 kg; solid potassium hydroxide 22.15 kg; deionized water 3.56 kg. Output: hydrogen 1.00 kg.

AEL is assessed over electrolytic-cell manufacturing and operation. Auxiliary BOP equipment, hydrogen storage and transport are excluded. The alkaline electrolysis system has a hydrogen-production capacity of 26 kg/h, a lifetime of 10 years, and 7500 h/y full-load operation.

Table 2. Life-cycle inventory of AEL for 1 kg H2.
Manufacturing: low-alloyed steel 6.62E-3 kg; nickel 1.03E-4 kg; chromium 1.03E-4 kg; aluminum 3.23E-5 kg; zirconium oxide 5.62E-5 kg; polysulfone 1.33E-5 kg; polyphenylene sulfide 1.74E-5 kg; polyethylene 2.56E-5 kg; synthetic rubber 3.81E-6 kg.
Operation: electricity 49.00 kWh; deionized water 11.20 kg; potassium hydroxide 8.50E-4 kg.

The paper reports GWP results of 87.78 kg CO2-eq/kg H2 for DACH and 30.76 kg CO2-eq/kg H2 for AEL. These impact results are validation context, not foreground inventory flows.
