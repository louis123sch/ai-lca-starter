[MAIN PAPER PROCESS DESCRIPTION]
Manufacturing starts with shredding rapeseed straw, then mixing with water and buckwheat husks, sterilization, inoculation, primary incubation, shredding, filling molds, secondary incubation, demolding/tertiary incubation and drying. The paper states that mycelium spawn culture was modeled as a subsystem based on Leiva et al. (2015). Background raw material activities and database proxies are supplied by ecoinvent.
