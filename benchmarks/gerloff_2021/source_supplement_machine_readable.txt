1. ADDITIONAL MANUFACTURING ENERGY

[PARAGRAPH 3]
Additional manufacturing energy for heat exchangers, pressure vessels and casing is based on literature. Containers for process equipment are assumed to need the same manufacturing energy as casing. Storage, hot water and buffer tanks use the same manufacturing energy as pressure vessels. Steel products such as pumps, tubes and other components are multiplied by 5.3 kWh/kg, aluminium and copper end products by 3.6 kWh/kg, and plastic products by 1.35 kWh/kg. Compressor manufacturing energy is estimated as 0.98 kWh/kg.

[PARAGRAPH 14]
General assumptions of single components
Control panel/electronics and transformer/rectifier are derived from Bareiss et al. and assumed applicable to the 1 MW systems where data are lacking. The transformer/rectifier weight is allocated 60% steel, 20% copper, 10% aluminium and 10% elastomers. The control panel/electronics is 100 kg and the selected ecoinvent process is electronics production for control unit RER.

[PARAGRAPH 15]
Diaphragm compressor
Due to lack of data, the diaphragm compressor including frequency converter and container with pipes and fittings according to Zhang et al., weighing around 2612 kg, is assumed for hydrogen compression in all 1 MW systems.

[PARAGRAPH 16]
Container and foundation
All electrolysis technologies are assumed to need a container including foundation. A 20 ft container weighs 2.25 t and a 40 ft container 3.74 t, made of low-alloyed steel. A 20 ft container foundation is around 5.6 t and a 40 ft foundation 11.4 t. For AEC the 40 ft container and foundation are scaled to 1 MW using a 0.7 scaling factor. A 20 ft container is used for PEMEC and assumed for SOEC.
