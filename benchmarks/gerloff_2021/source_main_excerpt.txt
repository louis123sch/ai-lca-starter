This study examines greener hydrogen production by water electrolysis. The most important water electrolysis technologies - AEC, PEMEC, and SOEC - are considered for a system size of 1 MW. Energy scenarios of Germany - 2019, 2030, 2050, and RE - are considered.

[PAGE 3]
2. Methodology
The methodology is divided into scope of the LCA study and water electrolysis technologies and generation of LCI data.

2.1 Scope of the LCA study
The allocation cut-off method is chosen as the system model of the LCA study from cradle to grave, assuming that the hydrogen production process is a single output process and the produced oxygen is released into the atmosphere.
The geographical reference Europe is selected for the processes. If no processes with the geographical reference Europe are available, RoW or GLO are chosen. Germany (DE) is chosen provided that the opportunity exists.
Two transport ways of the components are assumed: manufacturing/assembly delivery and transport to the water electrolysis plant. Each transport way is assumed to be 250 km for every component. Transportation with freight lorry >32 t, Euro 6 [RER], is selected.

[PAGE 4]
2.1.1 System boundary
The system boundary consists of the stack and BoP components of the 1 MW water electrolysis technologies and the different energy scenarios. Additional manufacturing energy is added. Processes for energy, raw materials, and auxiliaries originate from ecoinvent database version 3.5.

2.1.2 Lifetime
A lifetime of 20 years operating 8000 h per year is assumed for the electrolysis components without stack components. Stacks are replaced 3 times during the 20 years lifespan.

2.1.3 Functional unit
The reference flow corresponds to 1 kg H2. Component weights are divided by the produced amount of H2 in 20 years to obtain values relating to the functional unit of 1 kg H2. Stack component values are multiplied by a factor of 3 because 3 stack replacements are assumed.

2.1.5 Manufacturing energy
Additional manufacturing energy is added where ecoinvent processes do not cover the total manufacturing energy of components.

[PAGE 5]
2.1.6 Energy scenarios
Energy scenarios 2019, 2030, 2050, and RE are considered for Germany, with increasing shares of wind and solar energy.

2.1.7 Produced amount of H2
The production of 1 kg hydrogen requires 51.8 kWh (AEC), 54 kWh (PEMEC), and 42.3 kWh (SOEC). The systems are 1 MW, operate 8000 h/year, and have a 20-year lifetime. A 100% yield is assumed.

2.1.9 Disposal and treatment
Selected disposal processes from ecoinvent v3.5 are used because recycling and disposal processes are limited in the database.

2.2 Water electrolysis technologies and generation of LCI data

2.2.3 Solid oxide electrolysis cell (SOEC)
The SOEC BoP and stack data are collected and estimated using manufacturer offers, Zhang et al., Patyk et al., Hafele et al., and assumptions. A 7.13 MW SOEC source includes 11 heat exchangers and 5 hydrogen compressors; heat exchanger data are scaled to 1 MW and compressor data are assumed for 1 MW systems. Additional BoP components include mixer, splitter, heaters, water pump, separator, water purifier/feed tank, buffer tank and tubing. SOEC stack data are based on Hafele et al. and scaled from 1 kW to 1 MW. Some materials not available in ecoinvent are represented by proxy or self-created processes.
